Miami Center Ecommerce Redesign

Checkout funnel overhaul and iPhone 16 pre-order experience reducing 38% bounce rate and lifting conversions.

Category E-Commerce
Scope Web & Responsive
Client Miami Center
Year 2024
Objectives

Project Overview

Miami Center is a leading eCommerce platform in Egypt, specializing in mobile devices, with a focus on Apple products, tablets, laptops, and accessories, offering a shopping experience for tech enthusiasts.

Problem Discovery

Miami Center struggles with a high bounce rate by 38.29%

Customers were dropping off during browsing and cart stages due to cluttered product discovery and checkout friction. Addressing this friction required streamlining the buying journey, reducing form complexity, and improving mobile responsiveness.

Strategic UX Solution

Enhancing User Experience and Checkout for Better Conversions and Lower Bounce Rates

Competitor Analysis

Tradeline Leads in Engagement Metrics, While Dubai Phone Excels in Traffic and User Retention

Early Usability Testing

Why Do Customers Abandon Their Carts and not complete the checkout process?

Testing early on checkout flows to answer the question of what is the best way to solve users' problems. As the Miami store has always campaigns for product discounts, we tested 3 different checkout processes that depended on the old style to test what best checkout flow that make users complete the purchase.

Findings

To address the key issue of users not completing their purchases, we tested three versions of the checkout process through targeted campaigns. Each campaign directed users to a different checkout version whenever they clicked a link to purchase a product. Overall, we achieved a 40% checkout completion rate.

Final Design

Delivering a website and mobile responsive for Miami

Measurable Impact

Key Takeaways

The project helped me learn more about research and analysis. I learned to create a clear user interface that is responsive. I also learned how to do usability testing better with users.
